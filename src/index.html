About

Hanzi Lens

A Chinese reading companion for Windows.

Works where you read

Selected-text lookup and region capture. Hover lookup and automatic subtitle rescanning are not available in this version.

Your text stays on your PC

Dictionary lookup, OCR, and installed sentence translation work offline. Screenshots are processed in memory. No paid translation service or API key is used.
